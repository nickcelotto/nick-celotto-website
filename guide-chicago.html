Day by day

Day 1 Friday, August 14 Wheaton

Flew out at 4:10pm and landed in Chicago by 6. Got to a friend's place a little after 8:30, then wandered Wheaton College once it had cleared out for the night - a campus feels different when it's basically empty. Asleep by midnight, a short trip already off to a good start.

Day 2 Saturday, August 15 Wheaton → Chicago

Drove into the city around 11 and grabbed lunch at Devil Dawgs before spending the afternoon on foot - the Riverwalk, the obligatory photo at the Bean, and a stop through the Chicago Cultural Center. Dinner was deep dish at Gino's East, which lived up to the hype, then back to Wheaton for a walk through downtown before calling it a night around 11:15.

Along the Chicago Riverwalk

The Chicago River

Day 3 Sunday, August 16 Chicago → New Haven

Took the train into the city in the morning and had lunch at Dove's Luncheonette. Spent the early afternoon making one last round through Chicago - Wrigley Field, one more look at the Bean, and a stop at F1 Arcade - before catching the train back to the airport by 3:30. Flew out at 7:40 and landed in New Haven around 10:30. Short trip, but a good one.
