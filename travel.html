What I shoot with

Camera

Body

Fujifilm X100VI

Fixed-lens compact · 40MP

The workhorse. A fixed 23mm lens forces a simpler way of shooting, and the color science is good enough straight out of camera that editing is barely necessary.

02

How I carry it

Bags

Carry-on

Carry-on Suitcase

Cabin size · Hard or soft shell

A standard carry-on that fits in the overhead bin. Clothes, shoes, and anything that doesn't need to be within reach during the day. Currently scoping an upgrade - this may change.

Day bag · Personal item

Nordace Siena Pro 17

Backpack · ~20L

Goes everywhere with me on travel days and every day of exploring. Fits the camera body, a water bottle, laptop, and a full day's worth of stuff. The dedicated camera compartment is the reason this bag works so well.

03

Camera extras

Camera Accessories

Storage · Photos

SD Card · 256GB V30

The main card. 256GB is enough headroom to shoot freely for a full trip without stressing about space.

Storage · Photos

SD Card · 128GB V30

The backup card. Swapped in when the 256GB fills up, or kept separate on longer trips so everything isn't on one card.

Transfer

SD Card Reader

USB-C

Offloads photos to the MacBook each evening. Fast read speeds matter when you're moving a lot of files.

Carry

Peak Design City Sling (2L)

For days when the full backpack is overkill. Just enough room for the camera and the essentials, worn across the body and out of the way.

Power

Batteries ×4

1× Fujifilm · 3× third-party

Four batteries total. The Fujifilm one is the main battery, three third-party backups. A full shooting day can burn through more than one, so extras stay in the bag at all times.

Power

Battery Charger

Single slot

Charges one battery at a time overnight. Not the fastest way to top off the whole kit, but simple and reliable.

04

Everything else

Tech & Travel Essentials

Tech

MacBook

For offloading cards, editing photos and footage, and staying on top of anything work-related on longer trips.

Tech

AirPods

Flights, trains, editing on the go. ANC makes a long-haul feel manageable. Never travel without them.

Power

Universal Travel Adapter

Works in most countries. The one thing you really cannot forget on an international trip - everything else can be bought locally.

Power

Power Bank

For long travel days and anywhere the outlet situation is uncertain. Keeps the phone and AirPods topped up on the move.

Hydration

Hydro Flask

Keeps water cold all day - matters more than you'd think when you're walking 20,000 steps in the heat. Refill at airports to skip the plastic bottles.

Protection

Backpack Rain Cover

Slips over the Nordace when it starts raining. Camera gear, MacBook, and everything else stays dry. Lives at the bottom of the bag.

Sleep

Sleep Mask

Overnight flights and hostel rooms with thin curtains. Small and weightless - genuinely one of the better quality-of-life items to bring.

Security

Padlock

For hostel lockers. Most hostels provide lockers but not locks - bring your own and you're covered anywhere.
