Day by day

Day 1 Friday, September 11 Frisco

Flew out at 12:30pm and landed in Dallas by 3:15. Got to a friend's place by 4, then dinner at Taco Joint with him, another friend, and his mom. Spent the evening at a varsity football game where his sister was cheerleading. Asleep around 1am.

Day 2 Saturday, September 12 Frisco → North Richland Hills

Started at The Star with a friend around 10, then tailgated an SMU game that afternoon with a few friends. Dinner was Korean food at Hanshin Pocha with another group of friends, followed by a late round of pickleball. Didn't get to bed until 5:30am - a long one, but a good one.

Day 3 Sunday, September 13 Dallas

Breakfast with a friend at Original Pancake House in the morning, then church with him at Watermark Community Church. Lunch with another friend out at the Galleria - In-N-Out, easily the highlight of the day food-wise - and dinner later with friends at Yorienn Chicken. Back to my friend's place in Frisco and asleep by 11:30.

Day 4 Monday, September 14 Frisco → New York

One last breakfast with a friend at Starwood Diner before heading to the airport. Flew out at 11am and landed back in New York by 3:15. Four days, a lot of friends, not enough sleep.
